    R_(x,y;q,a) = P_(q,a)(y) P_(q,a)(x)^*.

    d_(q,a)(x,y)=0

iff the tested channel cannot distinguish x and y modulo q/gcd(a,q).

For primitive a this is exactly x == y mod q.
